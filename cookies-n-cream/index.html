Cookies n' Cream Ice Cream

Just some good ol' ice cream... but it's cookies n' cream flavored! And you can make it in just 30 minutes! Perfect for that hot summer day.

← ‎ Back

Ingredients

Whole milk

Sugar

Heavy cream

Vanilla extract

Oreos

Steps

1

Mix together milk and sugar until sugar is dissolved.

2

Add heavy cream and vanilla extract and whisk.

3

Pour mixture into ice cream maker and churn for 20 minutes.

4

Add in Oreos and churn for 5-10 more minutes.

5

Transfer ice cream to a separate container and freeze for 2 hours

6

Enjoy!
