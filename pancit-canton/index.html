Pancit Canton

Classic Filipino dish made with delicious noodles, meat, and a variety of vegetables. Perfect for any time of the day...

← ‎ Back

Ingredients

Flour stick noodles

Cabbage

Chicken broth

Pork

Shrimp

Carrots

Snap peas

Chinese sausage

Celery

Parsley

Onion

Garlic

Soy sauce

Oyster sauce

Black pepper

Salt

Steps

1

Prepare the ice bath and blanch vegetables

2

Saute aromatics and brown the meat

3

Add liquid and cook the shrimp

4

Add noodles and finish the dish

5

Enjoy!
